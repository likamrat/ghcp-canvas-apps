Token guide

Tokens are pieces of text. Input is what the model reads, including messages, history, and tools. Output is what it generates. Input/output totals add up across requests; context measures the current window, not those totals.

Models are from your open (non-archived) sessions. Usage levels follow your saved thresholds. Parents stay visible to show where matching nested sessions belong.
